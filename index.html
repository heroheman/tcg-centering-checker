How to use
  • Drag lines or points. Hold Alt to move 10× finer; nudge the selected one with the arrow keys (Shift = coarse).
  • Points: 2 segments per axis, each outer, inner, inner, outer. The result is their average; segments may be slightly tilted.
  • Rotate: Q / E ±0.01°, with Shift ±0.1°, with Alt ±0.001°.
  • Loupe: L cycles off, corner, cursor. Zoom with the slider or the mouse wheel over the image.
  • Straighten: for cards photographed at an angle. The original is kept.
  • Camera: fit the card inside the frame; the photo is cropped around it and the lines start on the frame.
  • A new photo on a side that already has one creates a new card.

How it is calculated

The worse axis counts. Thresholds: edgegrading.com (PSA since 2025, BGS, SGC, EDGE), cgccards.com (CGC; back from grade 8 down is unpublished, assumed as PSA), taggrading.com (TAG, TCG back). At BGS the 10 is “Pristine”, Gem Mint is 9.5.

AOG scores 0 to 100: per axis a 5 % difference is free, each further percent costs one point. Off-center (OC) at 50 or less, or 2/3 to 1/3. “Total” weights front 100 %, back 70 %.
