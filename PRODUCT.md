# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primarily the owner, a TCG collector (Pokémon, One Piece and similar), who photographs cards and measures their centering before deciding whether and where to submit them for grading. Used on desktop with a mouse for precise work and on a phone for quick checks. Occasionally shared with other collectors, so it must be understandable without a manual, while not over-explaining.

## Product Purpose

A static, offline-capable web tool (GitHub Pages, no backend) to measure card centering from a photo: load or paste an image, straighten or perspective-correct it, place measurement lines or points on the outer card edge and the inner frame, and read the left/right and top/bottom ratios together with the best centering grade possible at PSA, CGC, BGS, SGC, TAG, EDGE and AOG. Multiple cards are kept locally in the browser. Success means a measurement the user trusts more than eyeballing, done in a minute.

## Brand Personality

Collector's workshop with a precision-tool character: a workbench and a caliper rather than a lab or a game. Three words: precise, tactile, unpretentious. The interface should feel like a well-made measuring instrument that a hobbyist cares about: confident numbers, measurement-scale details, warm materials, no showing off.

## Anti-references

- Gaming or neon looks: no Pokémon-bright colors, glows or TCG-style flashiness.
- Generic SaaS dashboards: no interchangeable card tiles, gradients, hero metrics.
- Grader or shop marketing: must not look like the website of a grading company or a card shop.

## Design Principles

1. The photo and the numbers are the product; chrome stays quiet around them.
2. Precision is felt: fine controls, magnification and exact readouts are first-class, not hidden.
3. Self-explanatory over explained: controls name what they do, help is available but folded away.
4. Works by hand on a phone as well as with a mouse on a desktop.
5. Honest results: show where numbers come from and when a grade is an assumption.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: sufficient contrast in light and dark themes, full keyboard operation, visible focus, respects prefers-reduced-motion. Measurement lines are distinguished by color and dash style.
